BioLans Privacy Policy

A team workspace for bioanalytical laboratories

Effective date: 14 November 2026

1. Who we are

BioLans is developed by Mohamed Ournani. For any privacy question, including a request to delete an account, contact mohamedournani50@gmail.com.

2. Who the app is for

BioLans is a workspace for laboratory teams. Access requires an account provided by the team’s manager. It is not a public self-signup service and is not directed at children.

3. Information we collect

We collect the information needed to operate the team workspace:

  • Account information: email address, name, and role in the team.
  • Team content entered by users: projects, method workflow data, daily protocols, notes, tasks, reagents and stock inventory, deep-freezer plasma inventory, LC column records, and laboratory presence on the lab map.
  • Article search queries: the drug or search terms you enter when looking up scientific articles.

We do not ask for payment card details in the app. We do not collect precise location beyond the presence a user chooses to show on the team’s laboratory map.

4. How we use this information

We use this information only to provide and maintain BioLans: to authenticate users, to show each team its own work, to sync data across devices, and to respond to support or deletion requests. We do not use this information for advertising.

5. Where data is stored

App data is stored in the cloud on Supabase infrastructure, which hosts the BioLans backend (database, authentication, and file storage used by the app). Members of the same laboratory team can see team-shared content, according to the permissions set for their role. Personal notes are kept for the account that created them.

6. Third parties

When you search for scientific articles, the search terms are sent to public literature services so that results can be returned. These services are:

  • PubMed / NCBI
  • Europe PMC
  • Crossref

Those services process the query under their own terms and privacy policies. BioLans does not include ads, does not sell personal or team data, and does not use a third-party analytics SDK.

7. Retention and account deletion

We keep account and team data for as long as the account or the team workspace remains active. To request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it, email mohamedournani50@gmail.com from the address on the account. We will delete or anonymize that personal data unless a limited record must be kept to complete a request or to meet a legal obligation. Team content that other members still need (for example a shared protocol or inventory record) may remain in the team workspace until the team’s manager asks for it to be removed.

8. Security

Access to BioLans requires a team-issued account. Data in transit is sent over HTTPS. Please keep your sign-in details confidential and tell your team’s manager or us if you believe your account has been misused.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Where a change is significant, we will also notify users through the app or by email when that is reasonably possible.
